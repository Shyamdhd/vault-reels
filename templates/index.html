Install Vault Directly

Runs standalone in its own window with zero browser clutter.

On iPhone / iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom bar of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ➕.
  3. Tap Add at the top right. Vault will appear on your home screen!

On Windows / Mac / Chrome / Edge:

Click the Install App icon in your browser's address bar to run Vault in its own high-performance window.

Mobile Sharing Setup

The Core Vault Workflow:

Instagram → Share → Vault → Saved!

1. Android & Progressive Web App (PWA)

Install Vault via Chrome (tap "Install App"). Vault registers as a Web Share Target, so you can share any Reel directly from Instagram into Vault.

2. iOS Shortcuts (iPhone)

Create a simple Shortcut in the iOS Shortcuts App that accepts URLs from the Share Sheet and sends them to your Vault link:

http://localhost:3000/?share_url=[Shortcut Input]
